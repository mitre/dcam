DevSecOps Capability Model (DCaM)

DCaM is a framework that ensures project teams address all aspects of software engineering with practical, secure development practices. DCaM can be tailored to the team environment, and has been implemented by defense, civil and intelligence organizations.

Assess

Establish a baseline of Agile and DevSecOps maturity via a survey.

Decide

Decide what practices to augment based on project team baseline and targets.

Plan

Draft an adoption plan based on business needs, identified areas for improvement.

Educate

Explore what each practice entails and how related practices are connected.

Iterate

Reassess and refine as project team capabilities grow.
