Assess: Assess using DCaM

The DevSecOps Capability Model (DCaM) has a cross-functional team survey that measures a project team’s maturity across the DCaM domains: Collaboration, Pipeline Management, Development & Test, Security, and Operations. Each capability is examined through a sequence of Yes/No questions that begin with foundational practices and progress to more mature practices and behaviors. Survey respondents should include all cross-functional team members, including development, test, security, operations, and product owners.

The survey is administered via an online Lime Survey or via Excel spreadsheets. The online Lime Survey adapts based on role and project team maturity.

  1. The survey only offers the respondent questions that apply to their functional role.
  2. The survey only offers respondents questions about more mature capabilities if they answer Yes to a significant percentage of the prerequisite capabilities.

All questions are answered with a Yes/No response. The assessment typically takes between 30 -120 minutes per respondent, depending on how the survey is administered. After all responses are received, the DCaM experts compile the results to establish a project team baseline. The baseline highlights project team strengths and opportunities for improvement across the domains and provides a starting point for evidence-based next steps.

Project Team Point of Contact Responsibilities

  • Identify the team member(s) who will be taking the survey, ensuring all cross-functional roles are represented.
  • Determine, in coordination with the DCaM expert, the appropriate survey format, depending on the project team environment.
  • Confirm timeframes for the assessment.
  • Communicate timelines and monitor for survey completion.

DCaM Expert Responsibilities

  • Configure and administer the assessment, including providing instructions to the respondents.
  • Upon survey completion, compile and correlate the responses to determine the project team baseline.
  • Coordinate with the Project Team Point of Contact to share the project team baseline.

Decide: Decide what practices to augment

After completing the DCaM assessment, the project team reviews the assessment results. Many project team have “Spikey” maturity, where they achieve a high capability level in one domain, and a lower capability level in another domain. The goal in this step is to agree upon which practices and behaviors to tackle for improvement first.

The responses can guide a more in-depth assessment including interviews and inspection of DevSecOps artifacts and processes to shape decision making.

Project Team Point of Contact Responsibilities

  • Identify the strengths and opportunities for improvement across each domain.
  • Define the target capability levels for each domain. These targets may be linked to outcomes such as a desire for faster delivery or reduced security risk.
  • Prioritize and select practices and behaviors for improvement

DCaM Expert Responsibilities

  • Analyze survey responses to identify which capabilities a team is using, not using, or partially using.

Plan: Draft and implement an adoption plan

After deciding the target capability levels for each domain, the project team is ready to create a plan to reach the targets.

Capability levels are composed of one or more high-level capabilities that are further sub-divided into capabilities. These capabilities are interconnected and have prerequisites that map out how the capabilities build on each other. A target capability level can be decomposed into specific capabilities that must be in place. These capability prerequisites provide a ready-made plan to mature towards target practices.

At the lower levels, the capabilities are team orientated and are typically implemented by individual teams; at higher levels they require organizational support and change. At all levels, any growth through the levels requires management support at the highest levels, to support any organizational or policy changes required and to support the teams moving forward.

Project Team Point of Contact Responsibilities

  • Identify any external constraints (e.g., tooling, budgets, resources) or plan-enablement activities such as training.
  • Create an action plan, tailoring the capability prerequisites for the project team.
  • Communicate the plan with the project team.
  • Implement the plan.

DCaM Expert Responsibilities

  • Upon request, available to assist with plan creation.

Educate: Explore what each practice entails

Visit the DCaM Explorer to learn more about domains, high-level capabilities, capabilities, and associated prerequisites. Each capability contains a brief description and links to external references that provide more detail.

Project Team Point of Contact Responsibilities

  • Learn about capabilities, prerequisites, and external references

DCaM Expert Responsibilities

  • Upon request, available to assist with education.

Iterate: Reassess and refine

Project teams can reassess regularly to show progress against stated goals. This is recommended every 6 – 12 months until organizational goals are achieved.

Project Team Point of Contact Responsibilities

  • Schedule when to reassess the project team

DCaM Expert Responsibilities

  • Upon request, assist with scheduling an additional assessment.
