FAQ

Why did MITRE develop DCaM?
MITRE created DCaM in 2019 to give organizations a holistic DevSecOps view of practices and behaviors needed for successful DevSecOps adoption. The DCaM experts recognized that many models have a limited focus (e.g., focus on tooling, focus on single areas such as security). DCaM helps teams build a shared understanding of the process, people, and culture changes needed.
